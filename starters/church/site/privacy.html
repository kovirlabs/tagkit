Starter text: this is a plain-language example, not legal advice. Have someone qualified review it for your church and the services you use.

What we collect

This website has no ads, no tracking cookies, and no analytics. We only get information you choose to send us:

  • Your name, email, and message when you use the contact form or the connect card.
  • The names and ages of family members you list on the connect card.
  • Your prayer request, with your name only if you give it.
  • Your email address when you sign up for the weekly email.
  • Your gift details when you give online. Payment is handled by our giving partner; we never see your card number.

Who sees it

Our pastors and office staff, to answer you. Prayer requests go only to the people you choose on the form. Our forms are delivered by a form service and our gifts by a payment processor, which store what you send under their own privacy policies. We never sell, rent, or trade your information.

Children

We don't publish children's full names, and we don't post photos of children without a parent's written permission.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom. To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, write to office@example.org, and we'll do it within 30 days.
