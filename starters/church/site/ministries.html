Cedar Kids

Nursery through fifth grade, during both Sunday services. Kids sing, hear a Bible story told at their level, and make something to take home. Parents get a page each week with the story and a question to ask at dinner.

  • Nursery for babies and toddlers up to age 2
  • Preschool and kindergarten, ages 3 to 5
  • Grades 1 to 5, in two rooms
Safety: every leader is background-screened and trained, two adults are in every room, and children are released only to the adult holding the matching pickup tag.

Youth

Sixth through twelfth graders meet on Wednesdays from 6:30 to 8:30 PM in the youth room: dinner, a game that usually gets out of hand, and small groups by grade. There's a winter retreat in February and a service trip in July.

Small groups

Fourteen groups of eight to twelve people meet in homes on weeknights. Most follow the Sunday sermon; a few read a book together. Groups share a meal, pray for each other, and show up when someone needs help moving.

New groups start in January. Tell us your neighborhood and free evenings on the connect card and we'll match you.

Music

The choir sings at the 9:00 service and rehearses on Thursdays at 7:00 PM. The band leads the 11:00 service and rehearses on Sunday mornings at 9:45. Both are glad to hear from singers and players of any age.

Food pantry

The pantry in the old parsonage is open to anyone on Tuesdays from 4 to 6 PM and Saturdays from 10 to noon. No paperwork, no questions. Last year it served 2,900 household visits.

Most needed now: canned vegetables, stuffing mix, cooking oil, and diapers in sizes 4 to 6. Leave them in the bin in the lobby.
