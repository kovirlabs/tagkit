What we believe

The short version, in plain words. Ask a pastor for the long one.

God

We believe in one God, who made everything and holds it together, and who has always existed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus

We believe Jesus is God's Son, fully God and fully human. He lived the life we couldn't, died for our sins, rose from the dead, and will come again.

For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.

John 3:16

The Bible

We believe the Bible is God's word: true, trustworthy, and the final say on what we believe and how we live. That's why our sermons work through it book by book.

Grace

We believe nobody earns their way to God. Forgiveness and new life are gifts, received by trusting Jesus, and open to anyone.

The church

We believe the church is a family, not a building or an event. We baptize those who come to faith, share communion together on the first Sunday of each month, and carry each other's burdens the rest of the week.

How we live

We believe faith shows up in ordinary life: in honesty at work, patience at home, generosity with money, and care for neighbors who are poor, lonely, or new in town.
