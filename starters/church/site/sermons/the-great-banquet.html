The Great Banquet

Luke 14:15–24 · Pastor Naomi Carter · November 1, 2026

Watch the sermon Listen (MP3)

The passage

Go out to the roads and country lanes and compel them to come in, so that my house will be full.

Luke 14:23

Summary

A man prepares a feast and sends word that everything is ready. One by one the invited guests excuse themselves: a field to inspect, oxen to try out, a new marriage. None of the excuses is wicked. Each is an ordinary good thing that has quietly become more important than the invitation.

The host doesn't cancel. He sends his servant into the streets for people who never expected to be asked, and then out again, because there is still room. The table God sets is bigger than we assume, and the only way to miss it is to be too busy to come.

Questions for small groups

  1. Which of the three excuses sounds most like yours?
  2. Who is missing from our table at church, and what would it take to invite them?
  3. "There is still room." Where do you need to hear that this week?
