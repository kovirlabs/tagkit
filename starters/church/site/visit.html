Plan a visit

Walking into a new church can be awkward. Here's everything you'd want to know first.

Tell us you're coming

What a service is like

Services last about 70 minutes. We sing for twenty minutes, pray, hear a 30-minute sermon from the Bible, and share communion on the first Sunday of the month. Nobody will ask you to stand up, introduce yourself, or give money.

Service times

Can't come in person? The 11:00 service is streamed live and posted on the sermons page by Sunday evening.

Parking

Park in the main lot off Orchard Road. The row nearest the door is kept for first-time guests and for anyone who needs a short walk.

Access

The building is step-free, with accessible restrooms, large-print bulletins, and a hearing loop in the sanctuary. Ask any greeter for help.

Questions people ask

What should I wear?

Whatever you'd wear to meet a friend for lunch. You'll see jeans and you'll see ties; nobody minds either.

What about my kids?

Children are welcome to stay with you, or to join Cedar Kids. Check them in at the desk in the children's wing ten minutes early; you'll get a matching tag to pick them up. Every leader is background-screened.

Will I be asked for money?

No. There's an offering for members and regulars, and guests are told to let it pass by.

Do I have to believe what you believe?

No. Plenty of people here are still working out what they think. You can read what we believe, and bring your questions.

Can I take communion?

Communion is for anyone who trusts in Jesus, whatever church you come from. If that's not you yet, you're welcome to stay seated; nobody will notice.
