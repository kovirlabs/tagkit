Other ways to give

On Sunday

Put cash or a check in the offering, or in the box by the sanctuary doors. Envelopes are in the pews. Service times

By mail

Mail a check made out to Cedar Hill Community Church.

Bank bill pay

Set up the church as a payee with your bank and it will mail a check for free, once or every month. Use the church's address.

Stock and legacy gifts

Give appreciated stock, or remember the church in your will. Write to the office for details.

Giving statements for your taxes are mailed each January. Gifts are tax-deductible as allowed by law.
