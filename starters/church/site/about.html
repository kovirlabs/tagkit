About us

Worshiping and serving in Cedar Hill since 1958.

What we care about

We exist to love God, love our neighbors, and help each other grow as followers of Jesus. For the longer version, read what we believe.

Scripture at the center

We preach through whole books of the Bible, and we expect it to change us.

Every generation together

Toddlers and ninety-year-olds share the same room, the same table, and the same songs.

For the good of our town

A tenth of everything given here goes straight back out to neighbors in need.

Our story

Cedar Hill Community Church began in 1958, when eleven families started meeting in the grange hall on Orchard Road. They built the sanctuary themselves in 1963, added the children's wing in 1991, and opened the food pantry in the old parsonage in 2012. Today about 320 people gather here each Sunday.
