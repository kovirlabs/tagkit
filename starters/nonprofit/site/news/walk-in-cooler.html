A new walk-in cooler doubles our fresh food

By Marcus Webb, operations manager · October 15, 2026

For years we had to turn down fresh food. Farms and grocery stores offered us pallets of milk, greens, and fruit, and we had nowhere cold to keep them. That changed in September, when our new walk-in cooler was switched on.

What changes for shoppers

The cooler holds 12 pallets, twice what our old refrigerators held together. In its first month, fresh food at the Saturday Market went from 41% to 58% of everything we gave out.

Thank you

The cooler cost $64,000, paid for by a grant from the Riverbend Community Fund and 212 individual donors who gave to our spring appeal. Installation was donated by Mill Street Mechanical.

Next up: a refrigerated van, so Home Delivery can bring fresh food too. Help us get there.
