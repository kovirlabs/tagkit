Our winter coat drive is back

By Hana Sato, volunteer coordinator · November 2, 2026

Riverbend gets cold fast. Last winter, you gave 640 coats, and every one of them was gone by the second week of January. This year we're hoping for 800.

What we need most

  • Children's coats, sizes 4 to 14
  • Adult coats in large and extra large
  • New hats, gloves, and socks (we can't accept used ones)
Please: coats should be clean, with working zippers. If you wouldn't give it to a friend, it's probably time for textile recycling instead.

Where to drop off

Bring coats to 214 Mill Street, Monday to Friday from 9 to 4, or to the Saturday Market. There's also a bin in the lobby of Riverbend Public Library until December 13.

Where the coats go

Coats are handed out free at the Saturday Market from December 5, and our Home Delivery drivers bring them to neighbors who ask. Anything left in February goes to the Riverbend shelter.

My son outgrew his coat in October. Getting a new one here, one he picked himself, meant a lot to both of us.
