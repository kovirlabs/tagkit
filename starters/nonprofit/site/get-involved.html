Get involved

Six hundred neighbors give a few hours a month. There's a shift that fits your week.

Ways to help

Volunteer a shift

Stock shelves, greet shoppers, or check people out at the Saturday Market. Ages 14 and up.

Drive deliveries

Two hours on a Tuesday or Thursday, in your own car. We cover mileage on request.

Run a food drive

At work, school, or your place of worship. We lend the bins and pick up the food.

Your first shift

Arrive ten minutes early and ask for the shift lead at the front desk. Wear closed-toe shoes. We'll give you an apron, a name tag, and a ten-minute tour before you start.

Questions

How old do I need to be?

14 for market shifts, or any age with a parent. Delivery drivers need to be 18 with a valid license and insurance.

Is there a minimum number of hours?

No. Some people come every week; others come twice a year. Sign up for the shifts you can make.

Can I get community service hours?

Yes. We sign school and court service forms. Bring yours to the front desk at the end of your shift.

Can my company volunteer as a team?

Yes, for groups of up to twelve on weekday packing nights. Add your teammates in the form above, or write to us for larger groups.
