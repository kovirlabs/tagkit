Programs

Free food, no paperwork. Anyone who lives or works in Riverbend is welcome.

See the calendar Ask us a question

Saturday Market

Every Saturday from 9 to 12, our warehouse at 214 Mill Street becomes a free market. Pick up a cart, choose what your household will eat, and check out with a volunteer. There's no sign-up and no proof of income.

  • Fresh produce, eggs, milk, bread, and pantry staples every week
  • Halal, vegetarian, and gluten-free shelves
  • Diapers and personal care items on the first Saturday of the month
Tip: The line is shortest after 10:30. Food is restocked through the morning, so late shoppers don't miss out.

Weekend Bags

Every Friday during the school year, students at eleven Riverbend schools take home a bag of kid-friendly food for the weekend: cereal, milk boxes, fruit, and easy meals they can make themselves. Families sign up through the school counselor, and bags go home discreetly in backpacks.

For schools: Want Weekend Bags at your school? Write to us; we add new schools each August.

Home Delivery

Volunteer drivers bring a week of groceries to neighbors who can't get to the market: seniors, people with disabilities, and anyone recovering from illness. Deliveries happen on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

To sign yourself or someone else up, call (555) 010-0200 or use the contact form.

Waiting list: Home Delivery is full until new drivers join. Urgent requests are served first. Become a driver.
