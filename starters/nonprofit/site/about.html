About us

Neighbors feeding neighbors in Riverbend since 2009.

Our mission

Riverbend Food Share makes sure no one in our town goes hungry, and that getting help with food is as easy and as dignified as shopping with friends.

Dignity first

No forms to prove need, no lines outside. Shoppers choose their own food, like at any market.

Fresh, not just filling

More than half of what we give out is produce, dairy, or protein, bought from local farms when we can.

Run by the community

Four staff and more than 600 volunteers. A third of our volunteers once shopped at the market.

Our story

We started in 2009 as a folding table in the basement of the Mill Street church, handing out canned food to forty families after the paper mill closed. In 2016 we moved to our warehouse on Mill Street and opened the Saturday Market. Weekend Bags followed in 2018, and Home Delivery began in the spring of 2020, when our older shoppers couldn't safely come to us.
