What we collect

This website has no ads, no tracking cookies, and no analytics. We only get information you choose to send us:

  • Your name, email, and message when you use the contact or volunteer forms.
  • Your email address when you sign up for our monthly update.
  • Your gift details when you donate. Payment is handled by our payment partner; we never see your card number.

Who sees it

Our staff, to answer you. Our forms are delivered by a form service and our donations by a payment processor, which store what you send under their own privacy policies. We never sell, rent, or trade your information.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom. To see, correct, or delete what we hold about you, write to privacy@example.org, and we'll do it within 30 days.
