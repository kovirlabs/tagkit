Programs

Three ways we get food to people

Saturday Market

Fresh produce, dairy, and pantry staples every Saturday morning. Shop like any market; everything is free.

Weekend Bags

Kid-friendly food that goes home in backpacks every Friday from eleven local schools.

Home Delivery

Volunteer drivers bring a week of groceries to seniors and neighbors with disabilities.
